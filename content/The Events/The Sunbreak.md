A military operation organized by the [[Council Of Sorcerers]] and[[{Military Group}]] in an effort to overthrow [[The Academy]]'s leader along side other missions. This military operation seemed to be planned for a long time but it was still rushed. Trigger might have been the [[The Girl]]'s sealing or the [[The Binding]] spell for the [[The Ancient Ones|ancients]]. This operation was advertised to public as a heretic hunt and was supported widely by public. Military personnel consisted of soldiers and spell casters . While their motives were not clear it is known that there was 4 squads with different missions. It seems what made this operation possible was spies that council carefully placed before hand. there were multiple spies with some dating back almost half a decade. One of these spies opened the door for them and let them in. Even though they got in from a blind point and made surprise attack they failed drastically. [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]] knew some kind of a squad operations was bound to happen sooner or later which is the reason he sealed the girl and bind the ancients within the spy's information. He knew it would made them rush it. Squads got separated after breaking in, going after their missions. They had floor plans of academy from the spies. 


Wolf Squad: This squad's mission was to capture headmaster or kill him if he resists. They needed to hunt him like a wolf hence the name. This squad technically got the farthest but they were brutally murdered by high ranking individual of the academy. This person got promoted to a key military figure later on throughout the years. skeletons of the soldiers can still be found where they fought. 


Bear Squad: This squad's mission was to capture the girl at all costs. The Council of Sorcerer's had some inhumane plans in mind but they eased it to public but making this seem like a rescue mission. The name was to put to reinforce this idea. Bear like a mama bear protecting her cub. This squad got separated after a while and they were picked off by the [[{Arcana Bot}]].


Magpie Squad: This squad's mission was to loot and capture all the valuable tech they can find while military being focus. Hence the name magpie. Due to the Headmaster not caring that much and other researchers there caring a lot this squad was able to complete their mission and reach to the horse squad's location of extraction. Researchers resisted but they killed everyone. This is where the most bloodshed happened since [[The Automatons|Automatons]] was not here to save them. They were able to load a single militarized aerial vehicle and different types of guns and ammunitions but due to the disappearance of the horse squad and other squad's not showing up they went after the other squads. They were quickly killed after that by remaining automatons.


Horse Squad: This squad's mission was to infiltrate the [[{Tram System}]] and devise an escape route. They were the escape plan of the operation hence the horse name. Not much is known about this squad when magpie squad reached the extraction location tram was ready but horse squad was nowhere to be found. to this day not a single body is found and every member of the horse squad is still MIA. Their disappearance might have been done by Ancients ones but there isn't any evidence that supports this. 


Aftermath: After every member of the operations killed or neutralized headmaster ordered the everyone to rebuild the academy and fix it to a degree. This went on a while but after some time people figured out that even if they fixed they wouldn't be able to stay here anymore. So about half of them escaped. Since academy wasn't in a great shape at the time they were able to escape unharmed. After discovering their escape headmaster ordered everyone to leave the academy or face the consequences. It is not known that the escape triggered the headmaster to do this or it was already planned but some people did not want to leave so they hid. they built houses and devised ways to hide from the headmaster. [[The Velopolis]] was found by them. Since rebuilding halted academy is still in a ruined in most places. Focus was shifted to military after the sunbreak and new model of automatons were built.

Spell that is holding the[[The Ancient Ones|Ancient ones]] from coming to this world. Put by The [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]] using ancient knowledge and wisdom. Headmaster started to design this spell when he realized the deal he made could end the existence of every [[The Humans|human]] on earth. His plan was to take the wisdom of the ancients and seal them with a new spell. He partly succeeded but this new seal is not merely a band-aid compared to the previous one put by [[{The Mother Of The Girl}]]. It needs constant [[Arcana]] to hold the ancients and even then it can't actually stop their influence that good. Ancient ones anticipated resistance or trickery but not to this degree. Going this many years without a herald made them blind to the development of human race.


Bind was done near end of the Isolation Era but headmaster was planning the bind since [[The Girl]] was born. What actually triggered [[The Sunbreak]] was spy reporting the bind to the [[Council Of Sorcerers|sorcerer council]]. This made them rush their planning and attack. Since bind was fresh at the time of the sunbreak ancient one's rage and influence was much stronger than ever. This let them lash out the any humans they could find which was one of the squads at the sunbreak. 


Following the sunbreak headmaster started to sacrificed humans to the bind to feed it arcana. He also experimented on various things to find a more reliable or permanent solution. He tried to enhance the arcana he could harvest from humans. He tried to find different sources of arcana. Most of these experiments failed but they produce a lot of defects. Some of these still roam [[The Academy]]. 


These days bind is weakening once more and it is only a matter of time before it breaks. As the bind weakens so the ancient one's influence over the material plane gets stronger. This lets girl of the mother too communicate with the group this often.

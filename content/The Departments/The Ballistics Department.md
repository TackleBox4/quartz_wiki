Department where guns were being invented and where explosion could be heard every day and night. Except it wasn't. During blossom era this department was severely underfunded despite being one of the departments with lowest demands. People that were interested in military technologies wasn't more than a handful. This made it ideal for [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]]'s secret study hall where he study [[The Ancient Ones]] in peace. This changed after [[The Sunbreak]] as the main focus shifted largely to military. After this headmaster built his [[The Invisible Tower of Brilliance|invisible tower]] so he could work in peace. After this department was mostly occupied by largely [[The Automatons|V2s]] and some V1s remaining. 


Secret study hall contains a lot of information and documents about headmaster's past and his research. At the time of the blossom era his research was more about understanding ancient ones and bringing them back to this world. His notes can be divided into two as pre [[The Girl]] and after it. When party finds these notes it strengthens idea that he is the villain that will bring the end of the world and that he must be stopped. Information about the girl's implant and one of the only autodocs in working condition can also be found here.


Girl awakening quest happens in this location. Party comes here upon girl's idea of finding firepower to defend themselves better. Party battles through the V2 patrols and old V1s until they reach to the weapon armory of V2s. It is heavily guarded but they are able to infiltrate it. During one of the critical moments of the infiltration girl gets some kind of a vision. She spaces out and almost travels to some kind of distant place before returning. As they continue through the armory they see a prototype of a weapon being tested behind the glass. Later on party can find this room and take the gun for girl's arsenal. As they are continuing the armory they see a figure one that is different from the automatons. A human shaped figure with guns. [[Smithgun]] also can be found at the armory as a ridicule object of V2s. It can be taken back to the hub if party chooses so.


After getting out of armory and continuing a bit party can explore around to find a knight's armor laying to the wall. They thinks it's a corpse or someone's armor left and get closer to inspect it. they see no movement and no weapon. Armor itself it to bulky to carry so they decide to leave it alone. As they are leaving girl eyes lock in with another set of eyes behind the slit. Party draw their weapons in quick succession and [[Pretender Knight]] gets up as well. Knight warns them and tells them to back off or else. Party can choose to do so or attack and kill the knight. 


Later on they find the study hall in an accident. While they are traversing the department girl spaces out again this time for longer. She walks around this weird landscape where every step hurts. As she comes back she realizes she is trapped in a dark room. She calls out to the party in panic which they hear as they are not far away. A wall is between them. Girl searches around in dark to find some kind of a lever. After finding it party can go into the secret study hall. 


Here they find study notes and diary entries left by the headmaster. As they are searching around the documents girl finds out about her origins and implant that her father devised. As she is processing this new information she spaces out once more. This time by herself even though still involuntarily. She sees a city this time. In ruins. Agony and pain is everywhere in this place. She feels sick by just looking at it. At this point it isn't hard for her to deduce that this is the ancient ones realm. After a bit more she gets overwhelmed by the pain and suffering and she gets back to the earth. The moment she arrives she passes out. After a while she wakes up and sees the party trying to wake her up. They lay her unto the autodoc so that she would be comfortable. After this she realizes that she needs to find this implant so that she would be fixed. that's what the documents said. That she would be stabilized. Party leaves the study hall to retreat to the hub. 


As they are about the leave the department that man from the armory stops them and a boss fight happens. After it girl can acquire another weapon for her arsenal from his body. Party returns to [[The Hub]] to rest. As they are sitting by the[[The Robot]] suggest looking at the medicine department as that's where all bodies injured or dead are taken. He suggest that the fact they haven't seen a single body says that automaton is still in working condition. Party leaves for the [[The Medicine Department]] next day.

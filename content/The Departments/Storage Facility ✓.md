Storage facility of [[The Academy ✓]] where most items ends up sooner or later. It was highly effective at storing prototypes, inventions and equipment. It had a system where anyone could use to find any item they want in minutes. Tram lines were integrated into the department later on. Heavy duty cargo trams carried crates here all day. With big and countless shelves it is one of the biggest places in the academy. This was until [[The Sunbreak ✓]] of course.


During sunbreak magpie squad ransacked the place and after hijacking a cargo tram they were ready to get out. They were also ordered to pour this new black powder on the base of the shelves. after the explosions Storage facility was a mess. It was impossible to locate any item with the sorting system broken. 


Party comes here looking for the [[The Robot ✓]]'s legs upgrade. Ghost of this department is happy that the place is destroyed. Thinks these dangerous technology shouldn't be in anyone's hand. Party asks him about the legs. He says that the answer is still the same and refuses to answer. Then he realizes this is a different [[The Automatons ✓|Automaton]] asking and states that. This prior automaton is most likely [[The True Weapon]] searching for his parts. After learning that party is trying to dig up valuable technology he constantly tries to get in their way and tries to stop them. 


Before party can find the legs they need to repair the sorting system and locate the legs with it. While repairing party fight through all kinds of enemies. Prototypes stored in crates or experiments while also trying to avoid ghost's obstacles and distractions. After locating the legs they need to search around the piles of crates. After getting the legs ghost threatens the party and orders them to leave it. After party tries to fight him he flees and disappears. 


This department's main quest line is rather short but later on party come back here for different side quests. Also party can return after getting the legs to take care of the ghost of this department. He begs for mercy in his last moments but it is up to the player to decide if he is going to pass to the afterlife in peace or in a painful way.

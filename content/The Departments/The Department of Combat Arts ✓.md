Department were all kinds of combat arts were taught and practiced. People came here to learn martial arts, weapon combat and self defense measures. They were also trying to integrate weapons into the new technologies they invented. Researchers were more interested in new technologies and inventions rather than the combat techniques taught. After [[The Sunbreak ✓]] this place was used by [[The Headmaster ✓|Headmaster]] to create the perfect weapon against the [[The Ancient Ones ✓|Ancient ones]]. He started with [[The Automatons ✓|V2s]] as they were the latest automaton model at the time but moved on to the V1s later on as well. After both of these experiments giving negative results he stopped the experiments briefly. Until V3s were made. He tried a V3 model as an experiment after even that one failing he scattered parts around [[The Academy ✓]] and stopped the experiments completely.


Department is mostly unaffected by outside events so most of the equipment there is untouched. These days it is occupied by [[The Band of Bastards ✓]]. They use this place for their main hideout. Abundance of weapons in this place makes a good base for them. Player needs to come to this department for [[The Robot ✓]]'s final upgrade part. When they arrive at this department robot is in a really bad state. Only thing he remembers at this point is that. He must fight and get the parts. After getting close to the entrance of the department party is stopped by the henchmen of the band of bastards. They are threaten and ordered to leave but they persist and state that they must pass. Party then gets captured by them and they are taken inside the hideout. Here they see [[The Leader of The Band of Bastards ✓]] giving a speech. As he is speaking to the deserters and killers one of them in the crowd leaps to him and stabs him in the face. Dagger get slowly absorbed into his skull. Then the leader holds the attacker's head and shoves him to his body like he is hugging him violently. Man screams until he can't. After few seconds the leader drops him and a body without it's torso falls to the floor. Then the leader approaches the party and ask why are they here. After a bit of dialoguing party can either attack a few henchmen and flee or pay an entrance fee or accept the leader's offer. He offers a passage in exchange of a favor. If party chooses one of the two non violent options leader helps them and tells them more about the place. After party tells the leader that they are looking for a sword he shows them their arsenal which almost have all the weapons from the department but not that sword. Also he tells them there isn't much of a danger except a few broken V1s. Party leaves for the department.


As they explore the department and fight through a few broken V1s they realize they are being watched by the [[The True Weapon]]. He gives them a monologue about how it is a mercy to put down robot at this point and how he is just doing his job. He activates and sends them true weapon prototypes. V1s and V2s. V1s are same with the robot down to the every last detail. after battling through them part finally reaches the true weapon and fight him. after being defeated robot is almost gone and in the brink of madness. Here party can choose for the robot if he is not the main character. Robot can either become a human by the help of the ancient ones and be saved in the process or party can transfer his memories too the true weapon. This makes it possible for him to use the upgrades. If he chooses to transfer he needs to live with the true weapon inside his head. After the boss fight party returns to the hub.


This department can be visited from the main gate if band of bastards are not enemy. Otherwise party needs to find a shortcut from another floor. department needs to be revisited for some side quests.

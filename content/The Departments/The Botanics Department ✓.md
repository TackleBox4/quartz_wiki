Originally a big biosphere where any studies or experiment that was plant life related took place. Sometime after the [[The Automatons ✓|V2s]] manufacturing they settled here and started to use it this place as a base. Years of sitting around doing nothing turned this from a base to a city. To this day V2s are still live there in their own culture and society. This place is generally out of the bound for players as it is too dangerous to wander there but player will go there twice in the story.


First time is for taking [[The Robot ✓]]'s arm back from [[{The Leader of The V2s}]]. They get an intel from a friend that leader sends the arms to a workshop in the border for maintenance. That day is tomorrow. They plan a covert operation to take back the arm. Party knows that they cannot take on the whole department. There is simply too much of them but they can sneak on and take the arms. After defeating few V2s they finally reach the workshop where they find the [[{V2 maintenance automaton}]] . That's the boss fight. After the boss fight they take the arms but V2s from the center arrive and they escape swiftly in an action sequence. 


Second time is during the robot's solo quest. Party realizes they need an army to even have a chance. Robot goes to the botanics department to convince V2s to fight against the [[The Headmaster ✓|Headmaster]]. They only allow him to pass without fighting if he surrenders which he does. They take him to the citadel where he will answer to their leader.


This is first time player sees the citadel. V2s built this place into a real city and not just a rational on too. Buildings carry artistic feature and there seems to be areas for entertainment. Plants and their roots seem to grow inside buildings. Some of the plants thrived while others died. They use the academy zinc coin and they have basic societal norms. Their leader is an interesting character itself. His controversial opinion on awakened ones do raise some doubts but others seem to respect him and abide him. 


Before The leader even listens to him he has to complete a task for them. They send him to the outer edges of the biosphere to defeat [[Mutated Plants]] and erase the danger so city can grow. Plants are sentient and can talk. They state that this is their rightful home and that V2s are the invaders. Player can choose to get rid of them forcefully by fighting or convincing them to leave. If robot is successful in any way he gets an audience with the leader. Here he can use arguments he gathered throughout the game easily convince him but if he has no information that can help him he will have to be careful of what he says. If successful V2 army will fight alongside the party during the [[Last Battle]]. After either successfully convincing them to fight against the headmaster or not robot leaves the botanics department. If leader was not convinced he will still let him go as he is unarmed and meant no harmed.

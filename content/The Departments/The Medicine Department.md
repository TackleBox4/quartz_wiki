A medical faculty at the time of blessing era. Inventors and researchers here founded really great things. While outside society thought that sickness comes from the evil spirits they were able to found the evidence of microscopic life and their harm. Their researches led to a countless cures for sicknesses being founded. They also integrated [[The Automatons|Automatons]]s to the medical systems with autodocs and [[Microsurgeons]] and of course their state of the art collection automaton. This automaton was able to analyze all of the academy, detect injured or dead people, map out the viable path to them and collect and bring them to the medicine department. This was especially helpful as accidents happened every day in [[The Academy]]. This departments success kept on going till [[The Sunbreak]]. 


During sunbreak medicine department worked really hard to keep people alive. like the most V1 automatons autodocs were broken and was unable to treat people's injuries. There was no human personnel available as well. But [[Graveyard Keeper]] surprisingly was in a working order. It kept bringing more and more people into the department but since they were not being treated they started to die one by one. People that were in good condition realized this and tried to flee somewhere else but Graveyard Keeper kept bringing them back due to his programming. People died from their injuries or starvation. Overtime this department filled with bodies as Graveyard Keeper went crazy from rapid labor and the atmosphere. In his best capability he administered the halting gas that was invented by researchers. This gas was able to slow down metabolomic activity to one percent of what it is. This slowed down rotting incredibly but over the years bodies started to rot. To these days there is an intense smell that is strong that it keeps anyone away from the department. Graveyard keeper is collecting bodies and thinks he is keeping them safe from the war. 


Party goes here after [[The Ballistics Department]] with [[The Robot]]'s suggestion. They are looking for the corpse with the implant. As they go into the department smell of hundreds of rotten bodies engulf them. They vomit and bleed from their eyes. They also inhale the gas which weakens them further. As the gasses affects are not immediate need prolonged exposure they do not go into a coma. Close the entrance they can find a group of [[The Doomsday Cult|cultist]] visiting here. They are not hostile unless attacked or insulted. After talking they reveal that this is their holy site as it calms them to see the dead. Most likely due to the gas exposure. They will give the location of their chapel if party is open minded to their religion. After a brief walk they reach to the main atrium where bodies are stored. Piles of corpses fill the big room. smell is especially strong here. Here can be found [[{organic medicine monsters}]]. They split up to search countless bodies. After a bit of searching Graveyard Keeper returns from collecting another bounty. It ambushes them one by one. [[The Old Man]] and [[The Tinkerer]] get captured before they could react. [[The Girl]] crawls into a pile to hide and graveyard keeper ignores the robot since he doesn't interest him. After collecting those two he leaves with them swiftly. After seeing them leave robot rushes behind them to save them. it takes a while for girl to crawl out of the pile. Now party is split into three. 


Robot follows collector around and explores medicine departments back end while girl tries to find the source of this gas. Gas started to really affect her. Her visions blur and she starts to shift dimensions back and forth constantly. This persists until she finally ends up at [[The Realm of The Ancients|The Ancient One's realm]]. A voice talks to her there. A woman. She seems calms and collected but her voice also sounds at edge like she is having difficulty talking. She talks about her body's incapability and her weak anatomy. She realizes this is her [[{The Mother Of The Girl}|mother]]. They talk a bit where she tries to convince her to accept her origins and save her race. After getting back to the world she is able to push to the room where gas is being ventilated into the department and shut it down permanently. This make Graveyard Keeper go into an alarm mode where he rushes to the main atrium also giving the robot the chance he was looking for the save the other two. 


Party meets back at the main atrium. Due to years of halted activity returning in minutes all corpses melted away and left their skeletons. Upon seeing this Graveyard keeper becomes enraged and attacks the party. After a boss fight they search briefly and find the implant. With the bodies being gone it is easy to spot it among the bones. 


Ghost of this department can be found at the entrance trying to warn people and even scare them away from the department. He believes that if people are kept away from this place he would be safe from the Graveyard Keeper. He attacks the party but lets them go later on after seeing how determined they are. He is still at unease even after defeating the Graveyard Keeper. For his quest to be done Graveyard Keeper needs to be repaired back to working order and goes into the cleanup procedure so that department can be habitable once more.

Department created by brilliant minds that also thought other life forms could exist or may have existed before. [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]] was afraid of others finding out about [[The Ancient Ones|Ancient ones]] but due to his school's whole thing being All info for everyone he couldn't outright shut the department down. People who worked there took a different approach to the matter than headmaster. They thought they could create an ancient one. They of course didn't know what ancients ones were or even the name "ancient ones" but they had clues and thought they could replicate these lifeforms. They created deformed lifeforms. abominations that didn't belonged to this world. Most of these forms didn't survive long but they got better and better at creating them. This continued until the isolation period where headmaster though department was getting too dangerous so he ordered it to be evacuated and exploded it's entrances with dynamite.


After this most of the researchers there left just like most humans at [[The Academy]]. The professor that pushed the creation of the department refused to leave and asked for a transfer to another department. Before he could be reassigned he was gone. He actually sneaked back in to the blocked department. He continued the experiments there in secrecy for nearly 10 years. He thought he was getting really close to create the perfect race. He learned to live among them and he started really care about them. He become obsessed with them. One day [[The Sunbreak]] happened. After hearing all gunshots and explosions he got afraid. He thought they would take , hurt and even kill his children. In a desperate attempt he injected himself with the solution. Soldiers never actually reached the department since it was blocked by the debris. To this day professor is still roaming the department protecting his experiments. He is a shell of what he used to be. 


Party will go to this department for [[The Old Man]]'s awakening quest. Department is mostly in a ruined state. Party will need to fight against different types of mutated creatures. They can find the notes, diaries and experiment logs left by the professor. After a certain point they reach the section where containment cells are kept. Some of the creatures are still inside but most is empty. Old man realizes a pattern here. Nearly every cell has a markings on walls or doors. What Some of them made with claws other with blood but origin seems same. He realizes this is [[Ancient Language]]. After a puzzle section he puts it together and reads it a loud with the help of the alter.


After this quest divides into two sections. Old man gets trapped in his own mind and tries to reach the alter. Turns out that sentence in ancient language is an incredibly powerful statement. It corrupts and basically makes a person braindead after reading. Alter is able to resist a bit longer but he is slowly getting erased. Old man needs to sever his connection with him before it spreads to him. while he is on the journey through his mind party is trying to escape from the huge mutate creature that is chasing them while carrying old man. They think alter is killing him so they are taking him to a contraption they saw on the way to there. A mind eraser. Sends powerful shocks to the brain stem to erase one's mind. Old man navigates through the mind as they are going. He sees his memories. Alter borrowed them and thinks he lived them even though he has no memories of it besides the ones he borrowed. Old man sees himself taking book and his days working on the sorcerer council tower. Place is in a bad state. The mind is being destroyed. it's like worlds colliding. After he finally reaches the alter he realizes he made peace with his death and slowly waiting it. He can choose to embrace him to save him which lets him wake up before party can erase his mind or he can wait for party to erase the alter. If second one is chosen alter wishes for old man to wait with him and states that he is afraid of dying alone. He sits with him until he disappears. 


After regardless of the outcome old man wakes up as the hulking creatures catches up to them. Party fights it and thanks to the help of the old man's new powers they defeat the creature.

The professor that sneaked in there trapped him inside a experiment failure but a nasty one. Almost a blob of flesh. He is just sitting there trapped. Party needs to find it by chance or by reading about it. After that they can get him out and help him pass on to the other life.

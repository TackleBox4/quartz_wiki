A small place where most of the general systems are controlled and observed. These systems include but not limited to: [[{Tram System}]], Raw Material Processing Center Inventory, Steam Reading and Directing, Hologram Systems.

Tram System: Tram system is controlled and maintained from here to a degree. Party needs to repair the main console and specific tracks needing repair to open fast travel for that place.


Raw Material Processing Center Inventory: This where what department need what and when of it was controlled. Requests would be sent from here to department and they would be shipped from there. Today this section's console seems to be badly damaged probably beyond repair. Most likely destroyed by [[The Velopolis]] to cover their tracks. 


Hologram Systems: This is where [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]] projects a hologram of himself in most of [[The Academy]]. This one is heavily guarded by obstacles. If party decides to blind the headmaster's eyes they need to travel here to destroy hologram systems.


Steam Reading and Directing: This is where [[The Robot]] can get his steam tank upgrade. This is the place where steam supply of the academy is created and redirected. Console is able to show the user the steam readings of the academy in a heat map. Steam can also be redirected into the main tank and party needs to do so since robot cannot use an empty tank. Ideally this redirection can be done automatically from the console but that function is broken beyond repair. Only other option is to someone to go into the main steam room and reroute it manually. Except steam room is close to a thousand degree. Only one that is able to go into the room is robot. He volunteers and goes in. It is impossible to see anything due to steam but with the party's directions he continues. His metal alloy plating slowly melts due to the heat. On the room there is also half melted maintenance [[The Automatons|V1s]] wandering aimlessly. Ghost of this department is trapped in these very pipes among the steam. After successfully rerouting the steam to the tank ghost also gets trapped in the tank with the steam. From that point onward she can talk to robot occasionally.

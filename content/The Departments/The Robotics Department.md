Section A: Maintenance Ward and Workshop
   Department where [[The Automatons|V1s]] and V2s were mass produced. It was designed and operated by [[Grandfather of The Tinkerer|grandfather]]. It changed into grandfather's prison and [[Ghost of Suffering|Demon]]'s hunting ground after his disobeying. This is where [[The Tinkerer]]'s awakening quest takes place. Party goes here to find a parts for a prosthesis for tinkerer. They come across hollowed and drained bodies. Department seems to be a really ominous place. Sure there are a few automatons usually broken V1s but there is a lingering feeling in the air. They feel as if they are being followed. Sometimes a shadow too dark in the corner of their eyes other times it's an unusual sound creeping closer. This is even worsened for tinkerer as he feels like there is a voice in his mind trying to speak to him. Offering him salvation but her very voice feels too much. It feels as his mind is exploding. This continues till they find the grandfather. Tinkerer doesn't recognize him immediately due to passed years and his current state. It seems time or something else has taken a toll on him. He is a shell of what he used to be. Tinkerer tries to make him remember but it's like he is almost on a trance. [[The Old Man]] thinks there is a spell put on him by [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]] and thinks he can break the spell under the right circumstances. 

   So they left that core room to find what old man needs but place seems to be different than it was before. As they pass a corner they see a rat. rat runs a bit after abruptly stopping. It starts to compress in itself almost. When party looks closer they see a shadowy figure looming over it. Now while doing the tasks they have to avoid this creature. Some of these things they need to do are behind puzzles or some enemies. After gathering everything they return to the core room to break the spell on the grandfather. That creature chases them into that room. While old man is preparing the ingredients other two are trying to help him as much as they can. While these are happening Tinkerer is in a trance state. [[The Ancient Ones|Ancient ones]] are talking to him. Offering to save his grandfather and a new arm and asking for nothing in return. Tinkerer knows that nothing is free on the world but their offer sound good too. As he snaps out of it old man has already started to apply it to grandfather. Even though old man's theory is not correct this solution works and breaks the bond of that creature from him. As this happens Creature materializes and party is able to fight it. 

   After a hard boss fight. Creature seems to be done but this is only the phase. While the fight was happening grandfather was working on something on the background. He finally asks tinkerer to trust him. at that point ancient ones whisper to him about how they can save the day and help him. He makes a choice here. Depending on his choice he get awakened and gets a new arm. Phase two begins with this move set. After a hard boss fight demon is defeated. In a desperate attempt to survive he tries to haunt the tinkerer and feed of off his doubts, fears and grieving. After realizing this is too much for itself, it gets destroyed but during this sequence if tinkerer has trusted grandfather he kills him in an accident. If instead ancient one is chosen grandfather can be taken back to [[The Hub]].


Section B: Invention Ward
   For [[The Robot]]'s torso party comes here and tries to find it in invention ward. This place is filled with unapproved and scrapped designs of automatons. [[The True Weapon]] is also among them pretending to be a prototype. Party comes across him for the first time here. He puts on an act of crazy but harmless automaton. Party ignores him. They also get more insight about automaton technology and it's origins here. depending on if this place was visited before section A or not demon might be hinted at with visual clues. 


During Tinkerer's solo quest grandfather returns here to get something to help in the [[Last Battle]] if he is alive by that point. This is the thing that he was working on to stop the headmaster before he was interrupted.

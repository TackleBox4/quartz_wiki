The library of [[The Academy]] where every book is stored. [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]] also hid his mind here. Used to be one of the most populated areas of the academy during the blossom era. Now it is mostly empty except [[The Archivist]] and some other [[The Automatons|Automatons]].


Party goes here after settling on [[The Hub]]. After making camp and sleeping for the night everyone wants to go to a different place. [[The Tinkerer]] wants to look for his grandfather while [[The Girl]] just wants to take revenge from his father.[[The Robot]] wants to learn his place in all this but doesn't know where to look for it.[[The Old Man]] interrupts them arguing and states that they need a map of this place. He thinks a library must exist inside an academy this big. He thinks they need to look for that place first. After saying library girl remembers some stuff from her past. She used to play in a place with lots of shelves and books. She tries to remember how to get the library from the lobby. Party follows the girl. 


During party's first visit to the grand library they will have to fight through few type of librarian automaton enemies and also archivist as a boss fight. Not long after entering the grand library they find it's ghost. They ask for help locating the archivist but she is in too much of a distress to even acknowledge their question. She rambles on and on about state of the library and thinks all the valuable information will be lost. She also states that if the archivist was working as intended none of this would have happened. Since this is party's first confrontation with any ghost they don't even realize she is dead and try to get her to safety. After going through the library, defeating enemies and solving a few puzzles they face the archivist. After a boss fight they find the maps section tracing his rails to it. Player can also return to ghost anytime after defeating the archivist. She will state that she is happy the archivist is gone and things will not be destroyed but also ask party to find some lost books. These can be found through out the game and after all of them is found and retrieved her quest will be complete and she will reward the player. 


Leading up to the boss fight and during it The girl will remember stuff from her past. Like how she used to run around the seemingly endless shelves and how she use to play with the archivist, riding his legs like a helter-skelter. She doesn't seem to bothered to see him in this state or put him down. Perhaps she realizes he needs to be put down or perhaps she simply doesn't care anymore.


Party's second visit happens during old man's solo quest where he rushes here to find headmaster's mind hidden. He needs to solve a grand puzzle that is layered in every part of the library to get in. If ghost is helped at this point she can give a hint but if not she is still in too much stress to help.

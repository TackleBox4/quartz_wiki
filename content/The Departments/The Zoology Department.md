The Department where all kinds of animals were present and studied upon. The man who recommended this department to [[The Headmaster]] was someone who was in love with nature and life upon it. Someone really dedicated on his ideals. After a few years of gathering animals around the world he realized that some of their animals and their habitat's were in danger. [[The Humans]] were destined to destroy some of these races. He started to work on measures to preserve animal life. He refused requests of animals for experiments. He was disliked in [[The Academy]] due to this.


One day a zoologist came to the academy. He was accepted as at that time people weren't turned back. He started to work on the zoology department. His practices were quite different than the current director of the department. He thought lives of animals could be sacrificed for the sake of the science. He rose in ranks really fast. He was also fulfilling the requests of other departments such as sending them animal test subjects. Soon enough there was a rivalry in management at the department. Some people sided by the outsider while other sided with the current director. In the end the current one won. After his defeat outsider lay low for a while. He started to work on some animal experiments in secret. He was fusing animals while injecting them with [[Arcana]] blood. These hybrids were abominations. Word got around quickly in the department and came to the director. He had no permission to expel a person so he went to him to confront him. He was thinking he could have a heart to heart with him but the argument got heated quickly and suddenly they were yelling. suddenly outsider hit him in the head with a blunt object in a fit of rage. He thought he killed the director and started to panic. Academy wasn't a good place to hide bodies so he fused to body with another animal to make him unrecognizable. He was secretly hoping this would help his research as well. With the director gone he was able to experiment with animals as he pleased.


These days department is full of these different animal hybrids. Most of them will attack anyone on sight. Place settled on some different jungle rules. A weirder version of the food chain has settled already. Hybrids here survived for years by hunting each other. Player has to traverse the department and face increasingly more chaotic enemies. Animal hybrids stop looking like animals and start to look like monsters. After a certain point The sadist man also started fuse animals with researchers working there. Player can find these human-animal hybrids. Player can also find the original director. He is fused with several animals and looks horrifying. He is able to talk unlike other human hybrids but he lost his other human features. He is also blind. Player can find him at few different locations. Players can also find the ultimate hybrid mass. hybrid of ten or more different animals. This things hard to look at without getting a headache. It cannot be killed and it is in constant pain and suffering. If party explores a bit they can also find alligator-wolf. A clean hybrid that came out of the experiments. after rescuing her she can be brought back to [[The Hub]]. 


This is also where party meets the [[The Lone Hunter]]. She is hunting the party as they are one of the first humanoid creatures she sees. After confronting her she leaves the party and can be found again later on in the department.


This department's ghost is the outsider that killed the previous director. Headmaster might have bind him as here as a punishment or maybe he was simply determined at his job while he was alive. He seems to be regretful of his action when party meets him and he is ready to make amends. whether this is an act or not is for party to decide. He helps party to navigate the department but he might deliberately skip places where his worse actions resides. Party gets a choice towards the end to judge him and decide his end. They might help him pass the afterlife peacefully or painfully or they might just not let him pass unto the afterlife as well. They have a choice to trap him inside one of his creations and leaving him to suffer eternally.

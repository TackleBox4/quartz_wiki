This department was used to be the leisure and entertainment section of [[The Academy ✓]]. It was filled with restaurants and bars. After the era of blossom this department was closed indefinitely as the lack of [[The Humans ✓]] made the use of this department obsolete. It stayed closed till [[The Sunbreak ✓]]. Few years after sunbreak a need for [[Arcana ✓]] arisen. This was done by sucking the arcana out of the human body and essentially  killing the host. [[The Headmaster ✓|Headmaster]] ran countless experiments to find new ways to get arcana or the amplify the existing methods. Research led to concrete evidence that a "gambler's high" can amplify the harvested arcana about 50 percent. Same effect can be achieve in adolescents with fair and carnival rides. So the harvest department was built. A casino at one side and a site with different attractions aimed at kids at the other. As this department was repurposed much later no ghost was assigned to it. 


After department was built every harvesting was being done here. every human kidnapped would wake up in here play games and win. As they win bigger and bigger they got more and more hyped up. After a certain point they would be accommodated to another room for their big prize where they were swiftly executed. Their bodies would be sent to the [[The Invisible Tower of Brilliance|tower]] where it can be harvested for arcana. [[The Clown and The Croupier ✓|CnC]] oversaw the whole process. A special [[The Automatons ✓|V2]] designed specifically for this job.


Party will be separated as they enter the department.[[The Robot ✓]] and [[The Girl ✓]] goes to the carnival while [[The Old Man ✓]] and [[The Tinkerer ✓]] goes to the casino. This department doesn't contain any enemies as it was designed to protect humans till a certain point. Gameplay jumps between two groups at times. with old man and tinkerer you play different games to gain chips which you can turn in at the counter to get useful items. with girl and robot you ride different kind of attractions. This one is more cinematic and focused more on the dialogue between two characters. The croupier is present at the casino while the clown is present at the carnival but as the party proceeds illusion starts to fade and personalities start to confuse departments and commands. While girl and robot is riding some kind of horror tunnel ride it suddenly stops. They get out of the ride into the service tunnels. this gets them out of the designed path and out of the sight of clown. At the service tunnels they get more insight about CnC's origins and the plans of the harvest department. They go on until they reach the harvest room where old man and the tinkerer can be found in a gambler's high state. party fight against the CnC. girl and robot tries to snap old man and the tinkerer out of it during the fight. The gambling mini games along with ability to gain chips and spend them will disappear after party defeats the CnC boss fight. This department as a whole is optional.


[[The Lone Hunter ✓]]'s daughter's corpse can also be found at the harvest room. This is where her quest can be resolved depending on the choices made.

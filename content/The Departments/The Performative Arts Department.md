Department that was created by people with artistic desires that were also open to changes and adapting to the new technology. They believed [[The Automatons|Automatons]] were the next big thing in the entertainment business and more specifically theater. They tried to give automatons entertainment functions such as dancing, performing tricks and singing. They were able to engineer [[Puppets]] which were able to do these things via [[The Humans|human]] control but their experiments failed on creating an autonomous entertainer automaton.


This department wasn't a targeted area during [[The Sunbreak]] so it is mostly intact but time wasn't kind to it. Today department has been taken over the mad entertainment automaton named [[Encore]]. It took control of the puppets and he is luring anyone that wanders here to inside and puts him on sadistic shows or plays before flaying them alive and taking their voice cords.


Player will visit this place for parts of [[The Robot]]. His head upgrade can be found here with the encore. Player will need to fight through different puppet variants (such as dancer, singer, performer, magician) and eventually reach encore. Encore will offer head if they agree to contest in his show and win. Party can agree or attack for a tough boss fight. If party agrees to play along they will perform against the encore in a performance show. They have to make some choices and play some mini games. If party wins boss fight is easier and they get an extra reward. 


The ghost of this department was terrified of encore killing everyone and putting a bad name performance art. So he possessed a puppet of his to try to get close to him and possibly stop him. During the performance players can observe one of the puppet juries acting quite differently than others. He gives party high points and encore low points whatever happens. Encore realizes this after some point and he orders other puppets too lynch him but he escapes out of the sight before party sees what happens to him. 


Later on his body can be found not far. He is caught and already lynched. He is in a bad state. He ask party for help. States that his mission was completed with the encore gone but due to puppet being too damaged he cannot leave the body. Girl suggests destroying the puppet but he says that would erase him out of existence. Party needs to exorcise him out of the body.

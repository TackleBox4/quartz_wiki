Chaos Book

An Artifact with Ancient origins. Looks like a standard book. Writings inside are incomprehensible to [[The Humans ✓]]. Language seems to be ancient origins as well. Seems to be created by a past herald of the [[The Ancient Ones ✓|Ancient ones]]. This particular herald filled this book like a diary. She was taught ancient language in dreams. The context of the book seems dangerous.

Centuries after it was created book was found by the [[The Old Man ✓]]. One of the key reasons for his condition seems to be the book. He tried to decipher it to learn its contents. Some of the word he said out loud changed him drastically. Created the alter that hated him. A manifestation of the ancient corruption. During the periods it had control alter wrote on the book. Wrote his feelings , his hatred into a journal. Material book seems to be made of is an unknown origin.

At least a part of the book seems to contain some kind of spells. only ancient language speaking people could cast these without making a fatal mistake. This is primary way old man fights. It is unknown if the book itself has non-physical properties and the writing is the one that makes it act dangerous or the book itself is cursed as well.

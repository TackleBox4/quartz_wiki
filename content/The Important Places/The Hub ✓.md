Originally the lobby of [[The Academy ✓]]. A big, spacious place built to welcome all into the academy. It was left in a bad state during the isolation era and even a worse one during the ruin era. Party finds it after a boss fight in a tired and hurt state and declare it their camp at the end of the chapter one. 

It starts as a basic camp with a bonfire but continuously gets upgraded as you do main and side quests. You physically see the tents and later on building being developed there as you recruit more npcs to the hub. Npcs provide two types of upgrades. You can find blueprint of structures and build them later on with the help of a builder npc or you directly find upgrades that are applied immediately. Hub becomes the party's home where they rest, upgrade, talk to each other and to the npcs , trade and revive if they die. This is not their only save point but this is their only revival point.

Npc population affects the end towards the chapter 3. People that you did not recruited meet terrible fates. This does not mean ones you did will not. Also allies will show up in the last battle if you recruited them to the hub. Eventually when it gets big enough they name the place:.........

Hub can be attacked by different groups at certain points in game and will be attacked towards the end game. Last one is scripted and it will destroy the hub also setting of the events of the chapter 3. This scripted attack is designed by the [[The Headmaster ✓|Headmaster]] in the hopes of breaking the party's will to fight and make them retreat. He is certain he can defeat them but he tries to avoid this for the prophecy.

A hidden province found by [[The Humans]] that were ordered to leave but refused. They settled soon after [[The Sunbreak]]. Settlement is located on a distant blocked of part of [[The Academy]]. They protect their town by some kind of a concealment spell. Hence the name Velopolis as in Veiled City. People of the city protected itself and the city by laying low and harsh laws against anyone that dangers it. City is run by a authoritarian council. Party comes and goes here a couple of times throughout the game.


Technology: Steam technology already existed around this time in academy but they built upon it as much as their situation allowed. They dug underground for water and coal. Academy was already built upon here by choice so minerals were plenty. They invented hydraulics and combustion engines but latter was not used in anything due to the noise. They were also some promising protocols designed and actually made to a point but they were latter shelved since council found them "endangering of city's citizens".

Concealment: Even though it is told like it is a spell what is actually hiding the settlement is nothing more than a chemical reaction. This substance was discovered shortly after sunbreak by some scholars that were escaping from the [[The Automatons|Automatons]]. Some vials broke during the chaos and this substance made them invisible to the automatons. Later on they reverse engineered this. This substance is applied to the perimeter of the settlement in circular fashion. it needs to be reapplied quite regularly as it's effect vanishes. Recipe of this substance is hold by a number of people in the Velopolis and it maybe their most well guarded secret. This substance can also be used on the fly as a makeshift smoke bomb. 


Culture: Two generations emerged from the velopolis in the span of 50 years. generation zero was the founders whom are mostly deceased and the ones who are not are elderly people. they are mostly in the council or guarding the secret of the concealment. Anyone that was left from [[LUWS ( Labor Union of Worker Society)]] was belonged to this generation but due harsh work and lifestyle of the LUWS people very few survived. generation one was the first generation. this generation was fed stories of how outside world is dangerous and evil. They are extremely scared of the outside world and most abide the laws as best they can. this generations mostly does heavy lifting of the velopolis. They can be found practically everywhere and they make the most of the population. generation two is newest generation and the current one. they are mostly children or teenagers and they don't fear the outside as much as their ancestors. Most of them are curious about it and some even rebel against the council to know more about the outside world. Years of living like this and being taught to live like this made velopolis citizen's really quiet and paranoid. they mostly stick to indoors and they talk slowly like they are whispering. City does not seem to have any festivals except velopolis's foundation day which is celebrated very tamely and quietly also. City uses Academy's issued zinc coin since it was mass produced at one point and can be found practically everywhere.


Significance: Velopolis is a mandatory section in the game and one that frequently pops up. It's plays a huge role for [[The Tinkerer]] and possibly for the party in the last battle. Few different questline emerges from or to velopolis. City has various merchant's and important npcs that can be talked. City does change at few points in story depending on other actions or player choices. In the end city's fate is decided by player's choices or actions.


Location: Velopolis is located in what used to be Academy's material processing and transferring  hub. A practical department more than an academic one. This hub used to have conveyer belts for transferring raw materials to others but most of those tunnels collapsed during the sunbreak and the ones that survived are intentionally collapsed by velopolis or was too insignificant to bother. Due to their location being a stockpile of raw materials, velopolis grew significantly in a short timeframe.

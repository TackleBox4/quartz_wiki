Founded by [[The Headmaster]] after getting kicked out of his previous academy grounds. Following his departure many of his disciples followed him along with members of the [[LUWS ( Labor Union of Worker Society)]]. With the help of the LUWS workers he built this academy with seemingly pure intentions. LUWS kept the academy running till the Era of ruin. Academy can be divided into three eras in history.

Era of blossom: The first years of the academy. Headmaster allowed all kinds of people into his teachings. The campus was a center of knowledge in the country. The word of it got around and it got fame really quickly. these years headmaster kept the research down low and focus around other arts. most of his unrelated inventions come from this era. This era ended when headmaster discovered something. A breakthrough in his research which led to him closing the academy. The new arrivals were rejected from this point forward. Some existing ones left too. Academy became forgotten in time.

20ish years

Era of isolation: After the closure of the academy headmaster gave himself in intense research. His newly born daughter was mostly neglected by him. This era didn't turn this place into ruin although it was a lot emptier after the closure. This era contained major technological advancements such as steam powered automatons, piston systems and early ballistics. tech difference started the show around this time. Very little is known aside from these due to the fact that most people are either dead. recorded documents were destroyed or taken and people that remained alive were killed during [[The Sunbreak]]. This era ended after headmaster bind [[The Ancient Ones]] and betraying their deal which in turn triggered the sunbreak. 

10ish years

Era of ruin: Era that started with the sunbreak and the one that currently continues. Changes were drastic between this era and the past one. all [[The Humans]] except a few were ordered to leave the academy or face the consequences. some left. some faced the consequences. about half of them were either to bound to the place and didn't had anywhere to go or had their reasons to come into to academy in the first place and couldn't left. People left hid from the robots and found a refuge in a department that was blocked from the main building at the sunbreak. they named it [[The Velopolis]]. without the human care academy slowly withered away. but technological advancements haven't stopped. due to the longer timeframe and urgency because of the sunbreak they passed the outside world too much in technological advancements. gap couldn't close anymore. most advancements were military related due to this fact. ones that weren't related to military was about [[Arcana]]. master operated a lot of experiments on humans and animals to find a way to secure the bind. to make it stronger. most of them didn't end well. Some gruesome creatures still roam these halls.

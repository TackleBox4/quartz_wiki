
## Core mechanic: steam

 Most skills generate steam.
 
 Certain skills reduce or vent steam.
 
 Higher steam generally improves the Robot’s efficiency.
 
 Some skills are only available within particular steam thresholds.
 
 Reaching maximum steam causes overheat.
 
 Overheat completely stuns the Robot for one turn.
 
 Normal recovery returns steam to 10%.
 
 Mods can alter recovery and cooldown behavior.
 
 One discussed mod changes the recovery point to 20% and affects damage resistance during cooldown.

## Cooldown-exclusive skills

 Certain skills are only available on the first turn immediately after recovering from overheat.

 If not used during that turn, the opportunity disappears.

 One planned burst skill:

   Allows multiple skills during the recovery turn.
  
   Ignores normal steam thresholds.
   
   Causes an extended three-turn cooldown afterward.

 Exact burst count—two or three actions—is not decided yet.

## Parrying

 Robot is the only party member who can parry.
 
 Parrying is a real-time reaction input during enemy attacks.
 
 Success is based purely on player timing.
 
 Valid attacks open a parry input window.
 
 Unparryable attacks do not allow the input.
 
 Missing a valid parry simply causes the Robot to take normal damage.
 
 No additional failure penalty.

## Steam and parrying

Default relationship:

 Taking an attack vents some steam.
 Successfully parrying builds steam.

A mod can reverse this:

 Taking damage builds steam.
 Parrying vents steam.

## Position roles

### Front

 Primary offensive swordsman.
 
 Access to his major sword attacks.
 
 More likely to be targeted.
 
 Successful reaction produces a normal parry.

### Reserve

 Loses most direct offensive sword abilities.
 
 Uses preparation skills.
 
 Can analyses enemy weak points.
 
 Successful parry becomes a parry plus deflect.
 
 Deflect produces a counterattack.
 
 Counterattack activates on-hit effects.

## Weak-point preparation

 Robot can analyses a selected enemy and reveal a weak point.
 
 Swap attacks and reserve counterattacks can damage it.

 He can enter the front early, but doing so before preparation produces less value.
 
 Moving him forward through another character also bypasses his prepared entry attack.

## Position cycle

 Enter reserve.
 
 Manage steam and use preparation skills.
 
 Analyses a target.
 
 Exploit weak points through deflect counters.
 
 Use a swapping attack to enter front.
 
 Convert the preparation into sword offense.

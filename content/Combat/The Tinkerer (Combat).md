
## Core mechanic: contraptions

 Most skills build turrets or other devices.

 Contraptions do not occupy party formation slots.

 They are deployed at the Tinkerer’s current front or reserve position.

 Existing contraptions remain in that position when he swaps.

 He has one shared maximum contraption capacity across:

   Both positions.
   
   All contraption types.

 Exceeding capacity removes the oldest active contraption.

 Exact base capacity is not locked.

 Contraption activation timing varies by type.

## Turrets

### Standard turret

 Acts after the Tinkerer finishes his turn.
 
 Fires at two random enemies.

### High-precision turret

 Acts after the Tinkerer finishes his turn.
 
 Fires at one selected enemy.
 
 Can target revealed weak points.

## Stun mine

 Constructed from scrap coiled inside a box.
 
 Attaches to the nearest upcoming enemy on the visible turn timeline.
 
 Activates when that enemy’s turn arrives.
 
 Completely skips that enemy’s turn.
 
 Consumed after activation.
 
 Frees its deployment slot when destroyed.

## Position roles

### Front

 Defensive construction.
 
 Protects the pair.
 
 Interferes with incoming attacks.
 
 Can draw enemy attention.

### Reserve

 Offensive construction.
 
 Turrets and other damage-focused devices.
 
 Builds battlefield presence from relative safety.

## Position-changing contraptions

### Fog machine

 Moves Tinkerer from front into reserve.
 
 Makes that entire pair untargetable for a limited number of turns.
 
 Prevents direct targeting only.
 
 Area-of-effect attacks can still hit the pair.

### Noise contraption

 Moves Tinkerer from reserve into front.
 
 Taunts enemies.
 
 Causes enemies to target him for a limited number of turns.
 
 Places him forward where his defensive contraptions are available.

## Base position cycle

 Build offensive machinery from reserve.
 
 Leave contraptions operating in their deployed position.
 
 Advance using the noise contraption.
 
 Draw attacks and use defensive construction.
 
 Retreat using the fog machine.
 
 Resume offensive construction from reserve.

## Ancient Arm awakening

 Mechanical contraptions become organic Ancient spawn.

 Spawn are stronger than the original devices.

 Additional spawn types are added.

 Combat identity moves toward organic summoning.

 Deployment capacity is intended to increase substantially.

 Exact direction remains open:

   Increased fixed limit.
   
   Potentially limitless spawning.


## Grotesque Robot Arm awakening

 Retains mechanical contraptions.

 Gains direct tendril attacks.

 Metal control allows faster construction.

 First contraption built each turn is free and does not end his action.

 Applies in both front and reserve.

 Possible turn structures:

   Build once, then attack.
   
   Build twice.

 Path shifts some emphasis away from pure deployment toward direct combat and action efficiency.

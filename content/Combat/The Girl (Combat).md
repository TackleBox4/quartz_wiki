
## Core mechanic: weapon wheel

 Her wheel is her primary resource, action-availability and combo system.
 
 Player cannot change the layout of the wheel but wheel changes itself as new weapons are introduced.
 
 It returns to the default layout after combat.
 
 The active wheel position determines the current weapon.
 
 Each weapon provides a selection of associated skills.
 
 Some skills have additional prerequisites.
 
 Every weapon can hold a limited amount of skills.
 
 These skills can be configured to decide which ones are going to be available.
 
 The player cannot freely select any learned firearm skill at any time.

## Wheel movement

 A normal skill rotates the wheel forward by one position.

 Individual skills may but are not limited to:

   Reverse the direction.
   
   Advance three positions.
   
   Stop the wheel for that turn.
   
   Move immediately to a particular weapon.

 Swapping formation position does not rotate the wheel.

 Skills used in front or reserve still manipulate it normally.

## Combo positions

 Some wheel positions have no directly selectable skills.
 
 They still trigger contextual effects.
 
 Their effect may depend on which weapon preceded them.
 
 After resolving, the wheel rotates once as normal.
 
 They function as transition/combo positions rather than dead turns.

## Position roles

### Front

 Main direct firepower.
 
 Close-range orientation.
 
 Large weapons.
 
 Heavy single-target damage.

### Reserve

 Supporting and suppressing fire.
 
 Wider view of the battlefield.
 
 Multi-target and area-of-effect weapons.
 
 Weak-point support through weapons such as the sniper counterpart.
 
 Can route the wheel while preparing to return to the front.

## Position-changing attacks

 A recoil-heavy area weapon pushes her from front into reserve.
 
 A Max Payne-style leaping attack moves her from reserve into front.
 
 Her movement is connected to firearm behavior rather than an abstract stance change.

## Current combat rhythm

 Concentrated front-line fire.

 Recoil into reserve.
 
 Area damage, support and wheel routing.
 
 Diving attack back into the front.

The language [[The Ancient Ones ✓]] use to communicate each other. Heralds were taught this language via dreams. While [[The Humans ✓]] who have learned it thoroughly can speak, write and understand it that's not how ancient ones use this language. Ancient ones use their language together with their hivemind and their shared feelings. This concept is incomprehensible to humans. No written documents exist from the past except a diary of a past herald. A book that she wrote to keep record which later on is turned into the [[The Chaos Book ✓|Chaos Book]] by [[The Old Man ✓]]'s alter. Other humans in past did tried to learn it but their mind's shattered without the ancient one's guidance. Lucky ones went mad while others suffered worse fates. 


Old man is the only people that is known to learn the ancient language without an ancient one's aid. Ancient spells that are cast via ancient language exist but they should be only cast by high [[Arcana ✓]] blood sorcerers who can speak the ancient language. 


[[The Headmaster ✓|Headmaster]] was also taught the ancient language since he was a herald. He wrote his important documents with ancient language and even sometimes thought in ancient language in his mind. Later on the game old man has to navigate the headmaster's mind and his proficiency in ancient language help him massively.

Invention of a brilliant man that was obsessive with magnets. Thought he could use magnets as a source of energy and thought they were the next big thing. He tried to to convince [[The Headmaster ✓|Headmaster]] on his experimental design of the magnetic "horse carriers" and he succeeded to a degree. He was able to lay rails to pretty much every department due to rails being cheap to make but wagons were expensive with that funding only few was able to be made. This invention succeeded in the end but before he could achieve greater things [[The Sunbreak ✓]] happened and he was killed. most of his trams and and tunnels were also destroyed during the sunbreak. One of his trams was used by a squad during the sunbreak. That tram is still sitting filled with ammunition and an aerial vehicle.

Today most of the tram system is in a bad state but party can repair them and open them up one by one. This allows them to open the fast travel system for everywhere they visited. Tram system can be controlled from the [[Systems and Infrastructure ✓]].

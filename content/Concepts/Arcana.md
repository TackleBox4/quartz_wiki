An organic element that is found in blood. it is heavily related to spell casting. it's quantity is same in blood throughout the years however different in every person. it determines a person's ability to cast spells. being affected by spells is unrelated to the arcana. not much is known by outside society about arcana. Most of the information comes from [[The Academy]].


Cultural importance: Even though they didn't had the ability to calculate a person's arcana outside society highly regarded spellcasters. The ones that could perform harder spells were in higher positions. they based their classification system on this too. people that born with low arcana tried to replicate this spellcasting via high arcana infused blood. spellcasting with external arcana is highly unpredictable and chaotic unlike the real arcana using spells which are stable and consistent. it is also prohibited and seen as hereditary behavior. these black market arcana products were hunted. the people that supplied them and used them were seen as heretics and hunted as well. A [[Council Of Sorcerers]] decides a lot of things and oversees governments time to time. this council is consist of several high arcana sorcerers.


Significance in Academy: Academy pulled a lot of people that were interested in intricates of the spellcasting. Over the years they made significant discoveries related to arcana. became a prominent study topic in academy. Spellcasters existed in academy too but not in a hierarchical way like the outside. they were mostly used were tests and experiments. Before the closing people tried to share their discoveries with the outside world but they were either blocked or erased by council for certain reasons. Some of the products of these test are known to smuggled outside after the closing.


Classification: There two known classifications vastly different from each other. one being the prominent one in the outside world devised by the sorcerer council. this system relied one the person's ability to cast spells. it had 7 categories of spells seven being the highest grade. it lacked precision since it was based on what someone would cast not could. on the other side academy made devices that could calculate the arcana level on the blood. they ran tests on hundreds of people and made graph based on them. it had a threshold for casting spells. the academy's system revealed that most people cluster within a measurable range with no extreme outliers. This undermines the council's system directly.

The highly addictive drug discovered by people who were working on the plant growth solutions. The scientist were combining high [[Arcana]] blood with different minerals to get a solution that could speed up the growth of a plant. Jumpblood was discovered during one of the experiments. It didn't take too long to be smuggled outside of [[The Academy]]y and from there it exploded on the outside society. for a while it was everywhere. Then [[Council Of Sorcerers]] banned them calling it "devil's seed". Drug was still around but it was much harder to produce, sell, use and even carry. 

Jumpblood is a stimulant instead of a sedative. It finds the most prominent feature in the user and dials it up to the max. Due to this chaotic nature of the drug it was on high demand. Most people stated that it made them felt the exact way they wanted to do. Jumpblood can actually be useful if used right of course. Increasing the efficiency of the workers was a common thing done by bosses via spiking their food with jumpblood. [[LUWS ( Labor Union of Worker Society)]] fought against this for years. 

Inside the academy today jumpblood can still be found in various places. [[The Velopolis]] did not have banned it officially but it's use is heavily monitored due to it's uncontrollable nature. Party can get their hands on the jumpblood and use it to enhance their selves except [[The Robot]]. They can also get addicted to it and suffer withdrawals of it.

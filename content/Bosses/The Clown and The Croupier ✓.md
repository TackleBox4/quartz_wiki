The stationary V2 [[The Automatons ✓|Automaton]] that was overseeing [[The Harvest Department ✓]]. One of the only V2 models to be designed not to kill outright. They had two personality for two different sides. They changed personalities like masks and moved between the sides. They were tasked to entertain the sacrifices and ready them for harvest. At the end of the department they harvested them. At one point of their life they awakened after countless amounts of trickery they done and murder they committed. To not break down they tried to focus on the entertainment side on think of their job as "putting people to sleep". They kept repeating this and at some point they started to believe it completely. 


This automaton can be found on the harvest department. They ambush the party and separate the into two groups, [[The Girl ✓]] and [[The Robot ✓]] going to the children side while [[The Old Man ✓]] and [[The Tinkerer ✓]] going to the casino. During this whole sequence party interacts with them from the both sides. At the end of the department both sides end in the same room. The room where [[The Humans ✓]] are harvested. Party fights with the clown and the croupier here.

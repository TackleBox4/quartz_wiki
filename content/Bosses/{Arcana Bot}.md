A V1 [[The Automatons|Automaton]] that was a prototype on one of the experiments about combining automatons with [[Arcana]] to achieve a much more powerful model. This prototype and the whole experiment was later on scrapped due to failures and combining these concepts was deemed impossible.
Reason this experiment failed was that arcana needed to settle on birth. This was not an issue with [[The Humans]] as arcana was already assigned at birth to them. However automatons are not born so this was not replicable. So they thought. After one of the scrapped bot was deactivated it "refused" and returned to life. This was a rebirth moment which let the arcana settle. He was successfully integrated with arcana. He can cast spells and unlike humans his arcana levels are not stable and can be manipulated to go become higher via arcana transfusion. His physical appearance is jarring. He has bundles of flesh on several parts of his body. He is between human and automaton. He is aware that this appearance is quite undesirable and uses it to set fear on his preys. He believes he can achieve higher form, much higher than humans and automatons, by extracting and consuming arcana from humans so he hunts and kill every human he could find. He is disgusted by both humans and automatons. His existence shows [[The Robot]] that arcana alone is not enough to transform into a human. He can be found at the [[The Arcana Department]]. He is hostile to the group.

Title: Headmaster

Bio: He was an intelligent scholar. His teachers thought he had potential but his erratic behaviors and his lust for knowledge scared them so he was expelled from the institute. He went to open a new institute. Took his followers with him. Days passed. He turned smarter and smarter yet his lust for knowledge kept growing stronger until one day he found out things he shouldn’t. He found [[The Ancient Ones|the ancient species]] that used to rule the world. Any other weak-minded individual would crumble and submit to them. He kept his cool and stroked a terrible deal. He would gain the knowledge of the ancients in exchange for becoming their herald. This made him see a vision of some kind. A prophecy. Four figures were standing together. There was a young man and an older one. there was also a little girl and what seems to be an automaton. It was implied they end one race and free another but not which one is which. Ancient ones were immensely powerful, but they couldn’t influence the world without an outsider’s help. He didn’t want to see the world in ruins. Not because he cared. Because he knew he could do much better. So, he tricked the ancient ones and [[The Binding|bind]] them with a spell that would hold them for foreseeable future. While keeping them on the leash he tried to spread his knowledge as best as he could. He authored books and nurtured apprentices but one day his institute was closed and lost. No one heard from him again. These days the bind is getting weaker, and he knows that he has to do something about it. 

  
Ambition: Maintaining the spell that binds the ancients and possibly getting rid of them. Willing to do anything to keep them out. Ends justify the means.

Mastery: He spent all of his life pursuing knowledge. He mastered every craft he could get his hands on. He is not just smart. He is also cunning, charming and much more. He plans things way advanced. He is not just steps ahead; he built the path. He devised ways to prolong his life and extend this gift to people who he deems useful. 

Plan: He tries to influence the prophecy so that four people that come eventually would destroy the ancient ones. He also knows that this could backfire on himself. Tempting the fate one way actually make it go the other way. He readies for the prophecy in several ways. He seals [[The Girl]] so she wouldn't be the girl on the prophecy. he eventually makes [[The Automatons|V2s]] destroy V1s so that he could erase the chance that automaton being an unpredictable V1 rather than a V2. He also thinks that the old man in the prophecy is the [[Grandfather of The Tinkerer]] so he keeps him under his thumb. When eventually the suspected four people get together and forms the main party he realizes this is the prophecy. Watches the main party throughout the game via a hologram of himself. He doesn't try to hide instead he openly does it. This persist till they find a way to shut it down.

Foresight: Prophecy has to be averted to destroy the ancient ones as he cannot take the risk of not intervening. He also realizes that interventions might be already predicted beforehand and might be reason things are gonna happen the way they are gonna happen. He intervenes by avoiding or stalling instead of destroying or forcing. He tries to keep his influence minimal until the push comes to shove and his life is threatened.  

Weaknesses: Even though he understand systems and power dynamics he is emotionally blind. He can't read people that well. This does not mean he doesn't feel things. He misses his daughter or feels regret by certain actions but he thinks emotions of people can be controlled by him. 

Relationship with the girl and her mother: He probably knew that [[{The Mother Of The Girl}|girl's mother]] is the same ancient one that seals his own kind. Might be the reason he had the child with her in the first place. Girl's existence proves there is a way for ancients and [[The Humans]] to live in harmony but he knew that her mother's side would overcome her genes eventually so he designed a seal to stop her aging. As a failsafe he made an implement to protect her and stop her mother's genes from taking over. Another reasons would be that her brain would connect to the hivemind eventually which headmaster wanted to avoid and the fear that she could be the girl in the prophecy.

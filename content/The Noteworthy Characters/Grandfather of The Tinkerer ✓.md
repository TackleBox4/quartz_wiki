The great inventor of the [[The Automatons ✓|Automaton]] technology and the designer of the v1 and v2 model automatons. His inventions shaped [[The Academy ✓]]'s future. He started to work one the academy on it's early days. He was well known and respected at the time even by [[The Headmaster ✓]]. Most people thought he would be the successor of the headmaster and be the next headmaster. While working he visited his family often. Even after the closing of the academy headmaster trusted him enough to come and go. Until [[The Sunbreak ✓]] hit and work got a lot more busy. At this point he was scared of what his inventions could do and afraid of the headmaster but he still thought he could be swayed from this path. Until his son and daughter in law got anxious since they could not hear from him for a long time and decided to get in to the academy to see him. This resulted in getting them harvested and killed. She was late pregnant at this time. Baby was able to crawl out of the corpse with the help of the grandfather. Due to the unusual birth of this child he was missing his right arm from birth. He delivered the baby to the closest household he could find and went back to the academy. He knew child could not survive in the academy and he was to guilty to look after him. When he returned both headmaster and he was furious. from that point onward he basically became a prisoner for him. He was watched all times and even if he could get away his age was coming. Longevity spell was the only thing that was keeping him alive. However he did not gave up. he secretly worked on a invention that could be used to take him down. This was discovered not long after and he was punished for this. Not physically though. Headmaster told him that his grandson was captured days ago and harvested. Even showed him fake proof. That was enough to break him. From that day onward he did what he was told to like an automaton. 

He can be found on [[The Robotics Department ✓]], slaving away as usual. [[The Tinkerer ✓]]'s awakening quest is about finding his grandfather and trying to save him from that place. Seeing his grandson after this many years spent grieving him gives him hope but not enough to think clearly. Slaying his guardian and giving him hope is the basic structure of the tinkerer's awakening quest. At the end of the quest depending of the awakening path chosen grandfather can die in an accident or survive and come back to [[The Hub ✓]] with the party.

His existence helps with several quests in the future. He can help during the solo quest of the tinkerer and at the [[Last Battle]]. He does not survive till the end because of his age. Longevity spell severs after changing sides. He is actively aware of this and made his peace with it. He is happy to see his grandson once more and be done with his life after this long.

If he survived till the tinkerer's solo quest after he helps convincing the council he lefts for the robotics department. He states that there is another thing that can help in the battle. He goes to finish the invention he was working on to take down headmaster before. He will arrive during the last battle with it.

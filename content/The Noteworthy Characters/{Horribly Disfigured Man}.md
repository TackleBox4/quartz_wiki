Once [[The Humans|human]] that was experimented now a monstrous creature. his body is full of thick dark purple hair patches. He also has claws that seems to be an organic material of an unknown origin. He seems to move in monkey matter rather than a wolf. hunts humans. Has extra limb growing from his various parts of his body. some seem to be underdeveloped and just flesh blobs but some are capable of grabbing and pulling. His eyes are poked out by his claws. Done to himself in hopes to hurt no one else again. he also has a lot of injuries what seems to be claw marks.


Party takes his quest like any other monster contract but later meets him. He desperately asks the party to kill him.  Party can oblige and kill him and take the reward of the contract or ignore him and let him live also ignoring his destruction. Party can also capture him and put him in a cage in [[The Hub]]. from there his blood can be distilled to get rare resource. This is gets rid of the monster problem as well but it is like an agonizing torture to him. 


He can not communicate with speaking. He is like on the passenger side of a car watching himself hunt and kill people. Way he communicates with the party is writing. they find etching in the walls made with claws. Party figures out the story from the environment.

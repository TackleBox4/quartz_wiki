He was a normal man living in the outside world. A mere peasant. Not a good one at that. Stupid as bricks. Contributed nothing to the society. One of the reasons [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]] kidnapped him for the [[Arcana]] experiments. experiments changed him drastically. They seemed to be failing but unlike the other candidates he didn't die to experiments. this resulted in headmaster continuing the experiments on him, increasing the dosage of it every time. At some point he become unrecognizable. He was pure arcana. Experiments were finally resulted in something. Headmaster tried to extract arcana from him to strengthen the bind but at that point he was arcana in human shape. This much of it gave him extreme regenerative abilities. it was almost like a clump of magnets stuck together. they just reformed quickly after any kind of physical trauma. This made the harvesting impossible. After trying all kinds ways to dispose him headmaster just gave up and released him. This marked the end of arcana experiments. Unfortunately this dim man also experienced severe mental damage from the rapid experiments and headmaster continuously trying to kill him. He started believe he was chosen. Thought the voices inside of his head was higher beings talking to him. He has no idea what arcana or himself is. He just believes he is the powerful being that will led the humanity to salvation. Eventually criminals or fugitives that escape from the law to the academy gathered around him, respecting his power. He doesn't have capabilities of a leader but his power makes him one. To this day he leads them in academy. A bling shepherd to a herd of wolves.

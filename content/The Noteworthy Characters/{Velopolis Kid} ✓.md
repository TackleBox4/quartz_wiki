A 15 year old kid from [[The Velopolis ✓]]. Has a talent for engineering. Dreamt of seeing the outside world his whole life. He belongs to generation two of velopolis. His parents are executed by council for disobeying the laws but he does not know that. He thinks they died in an accident. Which is the reason he does not resent the council. He just tries to stay out of their way and gaze while working on his inventions. He worked on the movement protocol that was shelved secretly for a few years. The council does not know about it. 

As of today he is getting closer to completing the protocol. Until one day a group of outsider comes into the town. Since this is the first time someone comes from the outside he is incredibly curious of them. He even vouches for them against the council even if it does not mean much. Council is really afraid of the group since they recognize his daughter among them. He grows  closer to [[The Tinkerer ✓]] and sees him as a parental or brotherhood figure he didn't had. He looks up to him. 

He will help if velopolis is brought to the [[Last Battle]] and can die there fighting. This important for tinkerer's character arc. He gets along well with [[Grandfather of The Tinkerer ✓|grandfather]] as well. if he survives the last battle he can go explore the outside world and help people with his inventions or stay in the new academy and teach others his knowledge.

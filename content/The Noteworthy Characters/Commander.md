Originally a spy planted by [[Council Of Sorcerers]] to get info from [[The Academy]] but switched sides during [[The Sunbreak]] to join [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]] and become his commander. He believes in violence and carnage. Thrives in combat and war. highly skilled in close combat too. During the sunbreak headmaster promised him infinite resources to resumes his war. Alternate was them getting to the headmaster and capturing him. One path was zero bloodshed while other one was like a buffet. Commander took the deal and wiped the wolf squad. He serves the headmaster to this day as his right hand.  His body is full of scars in various places. He fights barehanded to get close to his victims. He loves to get drenched in blood and gets sexually aroused by violence.

He can be confronted and found multiple times throughout the game. some of these lead to fights. During the first fight he disobeys the headmaster's orders and initiates combat. he loses an eye and an arm in a defeat. These are replaced by robotics later on and this makes him stronger. In an encounter after this he can be convinced to switch sides. Promising him a bloody war to fight in return. This also means he loses the age longevity spell that headmaster put on him but he does not care as he plans this fight to be his last. Having a ruthless uncontrollable ally is dangerous and party may suffer the consequences later on.

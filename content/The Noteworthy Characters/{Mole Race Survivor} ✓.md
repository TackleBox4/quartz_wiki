One of the descendants of a prehistoric race that lived during the ancient one's time on earth. They were able to survive by living underground and hiding. They hid for centuries thinking [[The Ancient Ones ✓|Ancient ones]] never left. Most members of this race died in years due to lack of resources. Him and his family is possibly the only ones left. 


Centuries of living underground changed their bodies. They adapted to a new lifestyle. They are more mole like than [[The Humans ✓|human]]. They can dig extremely well but they have sensitivity to sunlight and a poor eyesight due to not seeing the sky for years. Their skin is filled with thick hair to keep debris and stones in dirt to hurt them. Their lack of resources made them really good at absorbing minerals from the ground and tree roots.


This specific member of the race is a lot more curious than the others. When he senses that there is a large closed building he think it could be a home to his family. Thinks they would be safe from the ancient ones there so he goes in to scout ahead for dangers. Some wanderers briefly see him and think that a mutated monster is roaming [[The Academy ✓]]. This is how the questline start, a monster sighting. When party inspects it they find him. 


He thinks they are the ancient ones that he needs to be afraid of. It takes a lot of convincing but he believes that they are not the ancient ones. If party choose to help him and his family he eventually moves to [[The Hub ✓]] and can be used for any digging job. He can be killed when first met and reward can be collected for him also.

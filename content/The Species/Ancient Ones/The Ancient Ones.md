An organic race that ruled before the mankind. Their erasure via [[The Sealing]] led to the [[The Humans|mankind]]'s rise. Every one of the members of this race is much more complex and advanced than any human. They rely on constant stimulation of their senses. They wither and die without if they go too long without stimulation but their senser are extremely blunt so they don't feel most things. This is the reason they adapted into a hivemind so that their perceiving of sense would sharpen thousand fold. For to survive they devised a cycle of violence. They harm and kill each other. Feeling of dying and killing combined keeps them stimulated. This cycle stopped after The Sealing. Their influence over the will of people and inorganic matter is immensely high. So much so that most humans can't even comprehend Ancient ones or anything related to them. however from their dimension their influence over the world and the people are very limited. They waited millenniums for an opportunity to take back the world. Their realm made them weaker and weaker to this day. Them unleashed into to this world would surely mean the end for the humanity but they can't get in back their selves. They need a human's help. A gatekeeper. About half century ago their gate was opened for the first time since The Sealing even though briefly but a it was closed again mere moments later by a strong bind in an act of betrayal. This bind is getting weaker and weaker every day.

Their Influence: They can go in to the dreams of humans and speak to them. Injecting them with ideals or goals. As the bind that is holding them getting weaker their influence gets stronger. It is said in legends that certain people can mutate and achieve their full potential but this seems to take toll for not just the giver but the receiver as well. Aside from these their influence is weak on this plane. Player characters will get offered these gifts and promises of more as the game progress and bind gets weaker. 

Their Motives: Their main goal seems to be survival. They do not seem to harbor any ill intensions a specific species. Their main reason to get back into earth seems to be related to their realm being painful and harder to survive. They see the earth as their birthright as they were the first ones on it. Even that their intentions may seem pure they will use every kind of manipulation and deception to get people to help with their return. Regardless of their motives their existence in this world would cease the existence of human race.

Their Culture: Very little is known about their culture as most is decayed or destroyed and few that remains is incomprehensible to humans. They seem communicate with each other via feelings instead of words almost like a hivemind. These feelings mostly exist as hate and anger for survival but [[The Girl]] is the living proof that survival via the stimulation of love. [[The Old Man]] is known to be one of those few that researched their culture extensively. 

Name "ancient ones" is likely just categorization made by discoverers of the races trails. what they are called in their own language is unknown but due to ancient language itself being incomprehensible it is probably better for it to stay that way.

Certain Ancient Ones play a major role in the game. Even though you can't see one with your eyes for 80 percent of the game their influence and aid can be felt all over the game. An example could be the [[{The Mother Of The Girl}]].

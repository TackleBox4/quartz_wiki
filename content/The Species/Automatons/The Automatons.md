Automatons are one of the primary species that exist in [[The Academy|Academy]] grounds. They are sentient hunks of metal designed for different tasks. They all are manmade. They divided into two groups after [[The Sunbreak]]: Pre-Sunbreak and Post-Sunbreak.

Pre-Sunbreak Automatons(V1): Most of them are designed for specific tasks. Productivity is prioritized over integrity. Their main goal was to help humans and support them in various ways. They were all over the academy. Years of negligence after Sunbreak caused most of them to fry their systems. Meant going crazy in robot terms. Crazy ones are one of the core reasons of academy's current state. Fried systems all had a different effect on automatons. Some become overly violent and attacked everything they saw. Some did their job in opposite which break the order in the academy. Some just collapsed as they shut down. Ones that haven't fried their systems tried to do their job but either they got into the way of the crazy ones or their step were misaligned which caused more chaos then there already was. Few of them survived to this day and managed to retain their sanity. Some say they still roam the academy grounds looking for crazy ones and putting them out of their misery. These series of automatons can be found in abundance in game. Nearly all as hostile.

Post-Sunbreak Automatons(V2): More advanced than the previous models this series of models were designed with mostly one specific task in mind: to kill or capture any unfortunate soul that wandered into the academy. These models were heavily monitored by [[The Headmaster]] as the high failure rates of the previous series. These series automatons were ahead of their time and a definitive upgrade in every way compared to the pre-sunbreak models. They could think and compromise as much as their mission allowed. They had basic a society and a culture. The few years following the sunbreak lot of adventurers tried to get into the academy this led to the heavy building and usage of these models but after the people stopped coming since people didn't return from here automatons got an abundance of free time. This led to the founding of the autonomous city in [[The Botanics Department]]. To this day these models live there and use that place for their head of operations. They can be found in game as enemies especially around the botanics department. To this day most answer to the headmaster. They hate the awakened ones. This hatred is even stronger than their hate for humans as they see them as betrayers of their races.

Post-Ruin Automatons(V3): The pinnacle of automatons technology. Designed by Headmaster. He cross referenced V1 and V2 designs and fixed their flaws. While V1 models were fully mechanical and V2 models were a hybrid of electric and mechanical V3s were fully electrical. Their CPUs were heavily protected so awakening was not possible for them. They were able to think and plan according to their tasks. They were machines designed for a task. No one knew their existence besides [[Commander]] and headmaster. Headmaster hid their existence from [[Grandfather of The Tinkerer]] deliberately as he no longer trusted him. V3s are used in the last battle against the party. Their exact numbers are unknown but they are much bigger in numbers than V1 and V2s combined. 

The Awakened Ones: the automatons that broke their code. there aren't many of them due to the fact that already few that awakened gets killed most of the time by the other automatons. Post-sunbreak ones are majority in this group due to the fact that their advanced systems has many more weak points than old ones. Pre-sunbreak ones are really rare and in this time their number is less than 5. The awakened ones become sentient following their awakening. For that reason their goals and motivations are vastly differ but their empathy for other bots remain. Even though they differ in goals and motivations they find meaning in finding other awakened ones due to the scarcity .This can be seen in the way they help each other and the insane bots. Player can meet few of them in game friendly or not. [[The Robot]] belongs to this group.

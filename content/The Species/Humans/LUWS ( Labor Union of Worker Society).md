Worker union that is found by workers and for workers. Fights for education and equal rights of workers. It's first presence predates [[The Academy]]. It was found by the workers who were denied education from academies. Later on they got a lot bigger and separated into different chapters. 
These chapters mostly did not get news from each other due to communication being hard and tedious. They mostly did their own things and had different rules. When [[The Headmaster|Headmaster]] was expelled from his previous academy he took two LUWS chapters with him and promised them everything they wanted if they help him built an academy. They would be allowed to be educated there just like any individual. So they agreed and joined him.

Throughout the Blossom and Isolation Era LUWS maintained the academy as best as they could. Some of their demands were met but not all. Due to most members actually living comfortably and getting what they want they stopped fighting for their cause. This resulted in disagreements in LUWS itself. Not long after this [[The Sunbreak]] happened. Most LUWS members were killed during The Sunbreak and who survived either left on headmaster orders or hid and built [[The Velopolis]].

While these two chapters live in the academy peacefully, [[Council Of Sorcerers]] already had enough of the other chapters so they started a countrywide heretic hunt for them. All chapters outside of the academy was demolished and the members were killed in public executions. After sunbreak, members who left the academy also were hunted by The Council. Ones who stayed are either very elderly by now living on velopolis or already passed away. If anyone from LUWS is still alive they would be inside The Academy.

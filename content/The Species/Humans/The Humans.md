Dominant race on earth since [[The Sealing]]. At the time of the events of the game firearms were just invented with gunpowder. The Humans are living big cities surrounded with walls. these cities make up countries. some countries have around 4 to 5 while others may have as much as 12. There are also people that live in countryside. These villages make most of the food of the cities. [[Arcana]] makes up a big part in their everyday life and society. Their religion is an arcana based one preached by [[Council Of Sorcerers]]. High arcana level people hold the power in their hand and shape the world. They have council of sorcerers who operate like a church. This council is founded by 12 high arcana sorcerers who captured low income people of the society with new promises. This made really powerful really quickly so most authorities just respect them and abide their wishes. This council has a one big tower outside of any city. They connect and speak with city's leaders here. Sometimes some members visits cities to talk or preach to people as well. 

The [[The Jumpblood|Drug]] is also illegal to carry, use and sell in big cities. This is rule highly enforced. even carrying materials to make the drug could be a reason for an arrest. 

[[The Academy]] is known by few people in the outside society. Those who remember [[The Sunbreak]] thinks academy was demolished but some criminals or adventurers know that it is still there in ruins.

[[The Ancient Ones]] are unknown to public. There are some bedside stories to scare the children or horror tales that could have been inspired by them but there is no actual info or belief on them. Those who take the stories too serious might have discovered the cults in the academy.


Even though Pre-ancient ones times were known as "no humans time" this is false to a degree. There was an [[{Mole Race Survivor}|pre historic race that lived in the underground]] like moles. These people had very different lifestyles and struggles. To claim that they are the ancestors of humans would be false as they seem to be an entirely different species. Not much is known about them since no one even knows they ever existed.

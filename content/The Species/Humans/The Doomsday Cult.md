A cult that is worshipping the [[The Ancient Ones|Ancient ones]] and praising their return to this world. Their knowledge is mostly what they have heard as rumors and some deducing. They do not know the real nature of the ancient ones or their realm but they understand that they are strong and their return would end life on earth. How this is interpreted changes between churches. There is three churches at the academy as of today. Their principles and ideals vastly differ from each other. while some may harbor ill intentions to the party and attack others may be more peaceful towards them. Some also despise other churches as well. They can be found at various places in academy and they have their own quest line where you can choose which church to back. They have rituals that differ between churches as well. Some may be more brutal while others are more of praying or meditating like. These people mostly found [[The Academy]] by outside rumors and made a myth themselves. These myths differed from each other in time and made the churches. They mostly believe ancient ones will bring salvation with their return and that we need to prepare ourselves for their arrival.

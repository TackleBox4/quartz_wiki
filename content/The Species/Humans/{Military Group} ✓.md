Military group that worked with the council during the sunbreak. They were the manpower during the sunbreak. They take contact from the cities. Council was a client to them too but they cut their ties with them since the sunbreak and are operating independently. Commander was also belonged to this group while he was a spy.

Party can find instructions of a communication spell on some squads corpses. Later on they use this to contract the military group. When military group learns that tram that magpie squad filled is still there, they ask for it in exchange of helping taking down the headmaster. Party can take the payload to hub to use it themselves or give to them gain a strong ally. 

Taking the deal comes with risks. During the last battle military group commences air strikes via the aerial vehicle they collected. They do not aim it specifically and don't care about friendly fire. They also send soldiers to fight against the headmaster during the last battle. This faction is also really hungry for power and wants academy's inventions on military and ballistics to get more powerful.

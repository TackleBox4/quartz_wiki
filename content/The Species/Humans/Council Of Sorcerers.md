Shadow government of the outside society. Highly regarded by public. They. control all the [[Arcana]] related stuff and hunt the who they deem "Heretics". Even though it may seem like they do not interfere with most matters actually they influence and downright decide most of the politics. This council consist of 12 sorcerers. Most are not known by public but some are public faces. Council is the on that came up with arcana measuring system of sorcerer ranks. They are afraid of anyone being able to hold high power so they are in some kind of a holy war against the black market arcana blood. Executions via bleeding out the victim are some kind of a ritual at this point. Since black market actually needs to get high arcana blood somewhere connection of them feeding the black market isn't hard to make. Since they are afraid of educated individuals they have a natural dislike of [[The Academy|Academy]]. They completely destroyed when academy tried to publish it's own method of measuring the arcana. they suppressed and silenced it until it was erased from the public. It was the natural move to ally with a strong military group to ready an infiltration. Spies was placed around these times , very first of them being The [[Commander]]. Even then they didn't openly had an action against the academy till [[The Sunbreak]]. After the failure that is the sunbreak they tried to make people forget about the sunbreak as basically no one was returned alive from it. They succeeded at this to a degree. Public forgot about it and they tried to forget as well after seeing academy being dormant for so long.

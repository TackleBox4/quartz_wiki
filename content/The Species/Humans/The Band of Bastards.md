Group of criminals that banded together in [[The Academy]]. These criminals escaped from the law to the academy after hearing about how unreachable it is by the government. Their number could be anywhere from 20 to 30 maybe even more. They are leaded by [[The Leader of The Band of Bastards]]. He keeps together and leads them so they don't slit each other's throats. They mostly rob and kill whoever they can find. They do not know about [[The Velopolis]] due to the concealment spell. They seem to wander from place to place and make temporary camps but it is suspected that they have an permanent hideout as well. Party can come across them in multiple sections of the game. Player can try to avoid confrontation, bribe them with info or resources or straight up fight them. These choices matter and can decide if [[The Hub]] gets raided by them or not in future. Leader has few people he trusts but rests are equal among them. They do not seem to have a definitive goal beyond surviving and thriving but their leader has an ultimate goal due to his visions and he is taking them along with him. They trust him and fear him because of his unusual powers.

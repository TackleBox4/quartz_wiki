#                                        Survivor ID Card
Name:

Title: Sparring Bot

Age: Unknown

Arcana: Null as he is not organic

Starting Location: [[The Department of Combat Arts]]/ Training grounds

Distinct Feature: An elegant steam machine designed for combat.

General Appearance:

Weakness: Anything electric. Also, property of the [[The Academy|school]] so sometimes it remembers it’s old duties and turns on the party. Adores organic life. 

Weapon Type: Training sword

Unique Talent: Can not get hurt by most hazards except electric. It is very skilled with sword.

Possible Awakenings: Slayer of [[The Ancient Ones]] or [[The Humans|human]] bag filled with Ancient Ones

Awakening Quest Location: 6 parts are scattered through various places in the academy.

BIO: A V1 designed by the [[Grandfather of The Tinkerer|tinkerer's grandfather]] to be a sparring bot for the combat arts department. Awakened suddenly after years of sleep. His memory core is badly damaged. He does not remember anything, only a few visions. His [[The Headmaster|Master]]’s voice in his receivers guiding him. He has different purpose than the other automatons here. His entire existence revolves around this purpose. He is not a simple sparring bot. He is the weapon that Headmaster was preparing all these years. He must end The Ancient Ones all together. But if he decides not to and goes astray nothing can stop it.

Upgrades: Upon initiating his awakening quest robot and the party has to collect all of the robot's upgrade pieces. there is 6 in total: The head, torso, arms, legs, sword and steam tank upgrades. they are in following places in order: [[The Performative Arts Department]], [[The Robotics Department]], [[The Botanics Department]], [[Storage Facility]] , Combat Arts Department and [[Systems and Infrastructure]].  They can be done in any order except the sword which will always be the last one. Here robot will face [[The True Weapon]] and learn about the secrets of his origin. Turns out robot is not special. there is hundreds more like him that was decommissioned due to chronic awakening problem in [[The Automatons|V1]] models. Later when V3s were invented there was one more model made and that one was successful. That one later turned out to be The True Weapon. Locations leading up to the sword all hint at that V3 and his origins. Some subtly and some more directly. That V3 will be met before collecting any parts and he will pretend to be an awakened prototype. Also robot deteriorates as he gets more parts. He starts to go mad just like other V1s. He slowly loses his memories. He thinks parts can fixes this so he looks for them. At the combat arts department he loses so much memory that he doesn't even know these people fighting by him or why they even fight for him. Party is aware of this but doesn't know it is caused by the V1 chronic problems until later. They try to help robot as best they can. Only thing he remembers that he must fight and get the parts. After defeating true weapon and getting al the parts he is almost gone. At the brink of madness but parts are not even compatible to him. Here he makes the choice to either be transferred to true weapon's body or accept the ancient one's offer and become human. This choice is either done by him if he is the main character or main character himself if he is not as he is not capable of making this choice.

Head: Performative arts department. taking there for personality and motive upload.

Torso: Robotics department. 

Arms: Botanics department. currently in possession of leader of the automatons.

Legs: Was known to be stored at the {Storage}. Current whereabouts are unknown

Sword: Combat arts department. 

Steam Tank: Systems and infrastructure. 

Slayer of The Ancient Ones: hunt the upgrades to become the weapon his master envisioned. He stays as a robot but not just a robot. A pinnacle of his own species. He becomes much more calculated and gain all kind of new abilities. All at the cost of his dream of becoming a human. ironically considering the other path choosing this path might be the most human thing he does.

Human bag filled with Ancient Ones: hunt the upgrades to become a human with the help of the ancient ones. They promise him humanhood but in the end he just becomes a husk of a human filled with ancients one's organic matter. Even though he got what he wanted in a way his farther than a human as ever he was before. However this grants him special abilities only ancient ones could do.

Solo Quest: Return back to the botanics garden to rally other automatons. Allies are severely needed for the last battle so this is an extreme measure. Going back there after taking his arm is seen as bravely stupid by them. Fact that he is an awakened one does not help.

#                                        Survivor ID Card

Name:

Title: [[The Chaos Book ✓|Chaos Book]] man/ Batshit dude

Age: Late forties

Arcana: Classification of outside puts him at a decent level but it is known that he doesn't cast higher spells to avoid attention of the council. True arcana level is unknown.

Starting Location:

Distinct Feature: Has the legendary Chaos Book. Also turns into to some crazy dude occasionally.

General Appearance:

Weakness: Narcoleptic. Also has dissociative identity disorder. Every time he sleeps his personality changes.

Weapon Type: Chaos Book. Can use scrolls to cast spells.

Unique Talent: Knows how to read (old language that no one speaks). Highly intelligent. Has a rare genetic mutation that allows him to increase his arcana over time but it is disabled due to the fact that half of his brain is occupied by an intruder.

Awakening Quest Location: The Department of Otherworldly Creatures

Possible Awakenings: Curing himself to limit break his arcana or embracing the chaos

BIO: Tried to study this ancient language and actually managed to learn it but he soon realized this language is too much for his feeble mind and he started to break. Before he succumbed to the madness he tried to cut his crazed parts of his brain like cutting an organ that has cancer. He managed to split his personality, but he remains and sometimes takes the wheel. He wrote the Chaos Book with his other persona. Nowadays he can’t really manage his other side. He came to this place to find a cure for his condition and figure out the meaning behind this book. 

The Arcana Grandmaster Path: Curing himself of the intruder in his mind. He loses ability to cast ancient spells. Disables the lock on his genetic mutation. Allows him to scale is arcana indefinitely in theory. These levels of arcana is allows spells never seen before but intense vibration of arcana in blood due to the high spells boils his blood. Even though his arcana has no limit his body can take only so much. This path is death instead of a cure from the alter's point of view. 

The Chaos Sorcerers Path: Embracing the stranger in his mind and coming the terms with him. Allows him to cast even stronger ancient spells. He makes peace with his alter. Terms being he gets to sit at the driver seat at times in exchange for not being total asshole an focusing on the mission. Choosing this path means old man recognizes the alter as an equal being with it's own right to exist rather than a sickness or a curse. 

Solo Quest: He goes into The grand library to find Headmaster's hidden mind palace. After he finds it he goes in looks for the source of his immunity. He needs to find it and make him mortal again. Headmaster hid his heart inside his soul and his soul inside his mind and hid his mind in a hidden section of the grand library. Old man needs to pass all of these sections and find the source of his immunity. His alter can help him depending on if he survived or not. He can also other means of help in headmaster's mind.

Conclusion: Depending of the ending he can run the academy as the new headmaster. He repairs the academy to a better state than the original one. Makes the academy as what it should have been: A center of information to all people. This allows technology to be shared with outside world.
Or choose to demolish the academy and getting rid of the cursed history of it. To make sure that no one else does something like this in the future. This in turn strengthen the influence of the council of the sorcerers and makes old man to go after them after the epilogue.

#                                        Survivor ID Card

Name:

Title: Girl Frozen in Time

Age: Physically 13, Biologically 60is

Arcana: Potentially higher than even headmaster due to both her father's and mother's origins. Her arcana is enhanced by her mother's side. 

Starting Location: Chamber Of Preservation

Distinct Feature: 60 year old trapped in the body of a 13 year old girl. Daughter of an [[The Ancient Ones ✓|ancient one]].

General Appearance:

Weakness: Her body and her height limits a lot of things she could but may prove useful. Has anger and daddy issues.

Weapon Type: 8-10 firearms with different uses acquired throughout the game. bench reactions vary for every weapon and her combat gimmick comes from her weapon wheel.

Unique Talent: she can go into tight spaces. She has memories and information from the past. Can dimension shift between our world and the realm of the ancient's.

Awakening Quest Location: Ballistic Department 

Possible Awakenings: either a gunjerer with implants or ancient gunslinger

BIO: Daughter of the [[The Headmaster ✓|Master]]. Grow up beside him in the school. She was ignored by him as long as she didn’t stand in his way. She spend a lot of her time exploring the school grounds. Since her father didn’t care too much, she was allowed almost everywhere. Days before The Sunbreak his father decided that it wasn’t safe for her daughter anymore here, but he couldn’t send her to someplace else either. So, she designed a pedestal to put her on. There no one could touch her. There no one could harm her. Not even time could effect her. She had to stay there until her time came. He made a mistake in the end. As the time passed her body didn’t age but she was awake and saw and felt everything for all those years. Suddenly she woke up trapped in the body of her younger self and she wants to take revenge on the guy who did this to her.

The gunjerer path: Path that her father envisioned for her. Surgically equipping an implement that is designed for her specifically to get conjuring abilities. implement fits unto the nervous system thus the operation and aftermath of it is a toll on the body. Because it goes unto the nervous system sedatives are not an option. scars never fully heal as well. first girl looks like a burn victim. gets better progressively. Might transform into a different look due to high arcana overflowing through them. Implement needs high arcana to be attuned but that is not the only condition. years of people trying to use it and dying tainted the device. now it only works on untainted people. girl being frozen for years make her the only available candidate. implement allows user to conjure various tools in need. a defensive measurement. in her case it is guns. Implement was likely a failsafe designed by headmaster in case the sealing broke but before he could implement it sunbreak happened so she was sealed in a hurry. Before she can choose to get the implement she needs to hunt it down from the others that tried to get it and died from it. Choosing this path means girl choose humanity and rejected her heritage even though how much it could hurt. Weapon wheel gets and addition of a weapon and becomes unbound which makes it possible to manipulate how it rotates.

The ancient gunslinger path: Path that her mother envisioned for her. accepting her ancient side and transforming into her true form. This transformation changes how she looks significantly. Her guns become organic and part of her. She can start to shift dimension on command. Her mother works hard to convince her to this path. Shows her the pain and suffering of her race. Tells her how she can feel their pain since ancient's are half hivemind. Basically calling her their savior. Upon transformation this pain get increased as well. so much so that she drops to her knees growling in pain but pain eventually becomes background noise as she gets used to the constant pain. Choosing this path means girl chose her people over humanity over a greater cause even if it might be a misplaced motivation. 

Solo Quest: She goes into the Headmaster's tower for spying. This lets her face her father after all these years. questline changes depending on her awakening choice. if she has chosen the ancient path father is much more antagonistic towards her and quest is harder. if she chooses the implant things are easier and her father is more welcoming. Quest's outcome decides how the assault will go and where will they attack specifically. It is obvious that she doesn't infiltrate his ranks but rather headmaster lets her in. He probably thinks he can control her even if it comes to it. This explain why he is suspicious of her actions regardless of the path she chose but he talks and acts more warmly towards one while being downright disgusted and upset by the other. Reason he let her infiltrate the tower in the first place is obvious. He probably misses his daughter even after all these years. Maybe he secretly hopes that she would changes sides. 

Conclusion: right before the epilogue she gets a chance to get back to her 13 year old mind to get her time back but also erasing all of the memories of the people she met after she woke up. Another option is to get her body synced to her age and becoming an old lady.

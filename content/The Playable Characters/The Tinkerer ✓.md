BIO: A tinkerer with a knack for robotics. Even as a kid he liked to devised contraptions from the scraps and used them as a toy. Missing his right arm from the birth. He knows that he is adopted by his current parents. He doesn't remember his father and mother though. Only his grandfather's face. Even though he did not know it his grandfather was the head mechanic in the academy. Also the reason his father and mother died and the reason he is alive currently. One day he dreamt his grandfather in old dilapidated building and when he woke up he knew where he needed to go.

Ancient arm: Acquired after accepting ancient one's offer. An anatomically perfect arm. Doesn't have nails and looks alien. Gives him abilities to enhance his turrets and machines. Choosing this path means he is accepting the offer of ancient ones over the design of his grandfather. however this choice lets him save his grandfather. This path makes him more of a summoner of ancient hybrid with metal creatures. Has touch related destroy/transform capabilities

Grotesque robot arm: Acquired after accepting grandfather's offer. Gives him a special metal that allows him to control other metals. This metal arm has a toll on his body. it corrupts and deforms the organic matter around it. It slowly spreads killing tinkerer. He is aware of this and in a constant fight internally but can't do much. Choosing this path kills tinkerer's grandfather shortly after in an accident. This pathway advanced his machines but also gives him brute abilities using his tendrils. if choose to tinkerer might later go after a vial to slow down or stop the spreading of the corruption.

Solo Quest: He needs to convince the council of velopolis to let them finish him the town's special movement protocol with help of the {velopolis kid}. This is important for the last battle. if he succeeds the velopolis is used as a mobile fortress in the assault to headmaster. if he does not party needs to go almost naked or find some sort of an alternative. From his trauma he feels like he needs to save these people. Some votes from the council can be gained before the quest either with doing certain quests on velopolis on chapter 2 or choosing the ancient arm and keeping grandfather alive.
